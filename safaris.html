SAMPLE ITINERARY

7-Day Full Northern Circuit

Day 1

Arrive Kilimanjaro Airport → Arusha

Briefing, gear check, overnight in Arusha.

Day 2

Arusha → Tarangire

Afternoon game drive among the baobabs and elephant herds.

Day 3

Tarangire → Lake Manyara

Morning drive, then transfer to the Manyara escarpment.

Day 4

Manyara → Serengeti (Central)

Long scenic drive through the Ngorongoro highlands into the Serengeti.

Day 5–6

Serengeti Full Days

Two full days of game drives across the central and northern plains.

Day 7

Serengeti → Ngorongoro Crater → Arusha

Crater floor drive, then transfer back for departure.
